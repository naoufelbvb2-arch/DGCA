# 12. Distinct-Mass and Multiplicity-Mass

Define:

\[
M_{distinct}(x)
=
|\{d:\exists e,\ d\in descriptors(e)\}|
\]

\[
M_{occ}(x)
=
\sum_e |descriptors(e)|
\]

Define repetition factor:

\[
Rep(x)
=
\frac{M_{occ}(x)}
{\max(1,M_{distinct}(x))}
\]

No threshold.

Report for:

- held-out;

# 35. Precompression Pair Similarity

For a held-out query transition pair \(\Pi_Q\) and a grounded occurrence
\(\Pi_j\), define:

\[
Sim_{pre}(\Pi_Q,\Pi_j)
=
\frac12
\left[

\right]

No threshold.

\[

\]

# 36. Retrieval-Relevant Compression Alias

For query transition \(t\), correct concept \(c^\*\), and wrong concept \(w\),
a retrieval-relevant compression alias exists iff:

1. the compressed directional transition identity \(t\) supports both
   \(c^\*\) and \(w\);
2. compressed sequence scoring gives the wrong concept at least as much
   contribution as the correct concept;
3. precompression evidence gives:

\[
PreMatch(t,c^\*)>PreMatch(t,w)
\]

Thus the precompression representation distinguishes the correct grounding
better, but compressed transition identity erases that distinction.

No similarity threshold is introduced.
